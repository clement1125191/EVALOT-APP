# Academic setup and fair marking

The interface uses educator terminology for lecturers and teachers. Profile role offers Lecturer first. Each subject/module assignment records an education level: Masters/postgraduate, University, TVET, Secondary, Primary, Pre-primary or Nursery. Suggested year/level/class choices are editable presets, not official academic regulations. Universities use Year, TVET uses Level, primary uses Primary classes, and the paper form offers suitable term/semester choices. Distinct academic stages are kept separate even when both use Year 1. The saved academic stage is passed to AI preparation, rather than treating every Year 1 paper as the same level. Existing assignments receive the migration default Secondary; review older university/TVET assignments in Schools & classes and select their correct education level.

Prepare paper has a submenu for Exam, CAT, Assessment, Homework, Assignment, Model questions and Edit/reuse past paper. Model questions use the existing PRACTICE assessment kind. Saved past papers are copied rather than overwritten. Uploaded external papers remain references unless an AI draft is explicitly requested with required materials and provider consent.

Marking submenu: Marking settings, Marking with camera, Review marking, Review marks sheet, Student marking drafts, Marking analysis and Grace marks. An optional UTF-8 CSV roster uses Registration number,Student name headers. Without it, captured papers create the student list. Identity suggestions may be checked against the roster, but are never automatically confirmed.

Evalot Fair Marking is the default and uses the supplied EVALOT_FAIR_MARKING_V1 policy. The uploaded 50-rule matrix is available under Custom rules; unmodified rules retain defaults. Percentages are unset unless explicitly supplied. Component credit from the question/guide takes precedence over arbitrary percentages. Maximum marks, original evidence, uncertainty review and educator final authority cannot be disabled. Policy and roster are locked once marking begins. Existing drafts and reviewed marks are never silently regraded after a settings change.

Each question now has a section, with legacy questions defaulting to Section A. Manual questions can assign Section A/B/etc; AI drafting and guide import request section labels. Paper previews, generated references, marks-sheet columns and Excel/CSV exports retain sections. The marking worker sends each section's question positions to the provider separately, checks source mappings and caps, and combines the drafts. Unreadable/unmapped answers retain a null suggestion and a review issue rather than an invented zero. Credit earned, credit lost and rules applied can appear in the draft explanation.

Analysis uses reviewed students only and reports the reviewed/captured count. Question and section averages, low-score rates, high/low student results and recorded issues are evidence to investigate, not automatic causal diagnoses. Difficulty calculations exclude recorded grace additions.

Grace marks are optional draft adjustments. Every captured student must be reviewed; if a roster exists every rostered student must be captured and reviewed, and open claims must be resolved. Failure means scoring below 50% of the targeted question/section's available marks. Educators choose a 70–100% failure threshold and a maximum added-mark amount. Preview shows each student's capped change; applying requires an explicit reason, confirmation and unchanged session revision. Marks never exceed question or paper maxima. Repeated requests use an idempotency key; a second allowance on the same target is rejected. Adjustments and original values are audited in append-only records. Published marks stay locked; claims remain the amendment route.

Deploy migrations 016 and 017 before serving the updated API. Existing teacher ownership and privacy boundaries remain in force. AI generation/marking still requires a configured provider; semantic grading quality must be evaluated on representative sample scripts. These features do not guarantee perfect marking or instant processing.
