# Evalot Teacher Workplace project charter

Status: proposed for sponsor approval. Version 1, 1 November 2026.

## Purpose and problem

Teachers prepare papers and marking guides, manage student scripts, review marking decisions and publish results. Evalot investigates whether a unified workspace can reduce repetitive work while preserving teacher control, marking fairness and usable records. The scale of the problem and actual time savings require teacher research.

## Scope

Included: teacher accounts and setup; manual preparation and optional AI drafts; school headers and paper library; correction workbooks and script capture; teacher review of proposed marks; publication and exports; audit and access controls. Features remain subject to baseline verification and configuration.

Excluded from the first pilot: autonomous high-stakes grading, student or parent portals, guaranteed CAMIS/SDMS integration, nationwide rollout, independent hardware development, and paid launch before entitlement/payment validation. Do not expand scope merely because an idea exists.

## Deliverables and acceptance

| Deliverable | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Teacher problem research | Interview summaries, baseline workflow, recurring problems and contrary evidence |
| Reproducible technical baseline | Recorded commands, environment, results, defects and source commit |
| Manual teacher workflow | Invented paper prepared, saved, reopened, printed and checked by a teacher |
| Correction workflow | Correct student mapping, reviewed uncertain answers, teacher overrides, final totals and reconciled export |
| Pilot readiness | Rights, permissions, privacy, deployment, support and restore checks approved |
| Pilot report | Measured outcomes, errors, teacher feedback, costs and proceed/revise/stop decision |

The desired 79 percent workload reduction is a hypothesis, not a measured claim. Measure preparation and correction separately against comparable manual tasks, include review and error correction time, and report sample size and conditions. Set acceptance thresholds after baseline research and before pilot testing.

## Resources and constraints

Budget: unapproved; record a cost estimate and spending ceiling before paid services. Weekly availability: Clement to record. Dependency owners: Clement to coordinate providers, hosting and teacher/site permissions. Keep only one development task active initially and use a weekly review to protect study time.

## Stage gates

G0 charter and rights review → G1 documented problem and feasibility → G2 tested workflow → G3 approved pilot readiness → G4 pilot evidence → G5 operate, license, continue development or close. Every gate records evidence, decision, approver, conditions and next review date. Return to earlier stages when evidence changes.
