# Public evidence and commercial policy

Project AS001. Proposed policy for founder review, 1 November 2026.

## Public description

Evalot Teacher Workplace is an Academia Startup project developing tools for teachers to prepare assessments, organise scripts, review assisted marking and publish approved results. Ishimwe Clement leads the work as a student project contributor. Teacher validation and operating readiness are evaluated through documented checks and a controlled pilot.

Do not claim a signed university partnership, established company ownership of all historical assets, verified handwriting accuracy, 79 percent savings, production readiness or a completed pilot without evidence.

## Evidence to show the world

Publish a concise problem statement, approved screenshots with invented data, a demonstration linked to the tested version, dated milestones, aggregate measured results and known limitations. Explain what is implemented, what requires configuration, what has passed controlled tests and what has been validated with teachers. Those are different levels of evidence.

Suggested case-study structure: problem → investigation → requirements → design decisions → tested prototype → teacher pilot → measured outcomes → limits and next steps. Retain genuine dates. The project existed before this pack; do not pretend these documents were approved before development began.

## Access controls

| Audience | Access | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Existing public repository and approved demonstrations | This repository is already public; documentation cannot make existing code confidential |
| Student contributors | Assigned work and necessary systems | Written role/rights terms, named accounts and minimum permissions |
| Teacher pilot | Their authorised workspace and pilot materials | Permissions, support, data rules and reviewed marking |
| Commercial evaluator | Selected demonstration and redacted evidence | Purpose, named recipients, expiry and confidentiality where appropriate |
| Operator or buyer | Only the rights/assets agreed | Due diligence, signed terms, payment conditions and handover |

Never commit secrets, signed personal agreements or identifiable scripts. Any change to repository visibility requires a separate decision; this pack changes no visibility setting. Public availability alone does not establish the specific licence rights offered. Review actual licensing and third-party obligations before promising exclusivity.

## What happens after a useful solution

The sponsor reviews pilot evidence and rights, then chooses internal operation, paid research/development, licensing, a funded partnership, a spinout, an asset transfer, continued research or closure. A twelve-month review may be useful but does not automatically produce a company.

| Arrangement | Other party provides | Possible Academia Startup benefit | Required record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Development services | Agreed inputs and milestone payments | Research/development fees | Scope, acceptance, costs and rights terms |
| Customer service | Payment and agreed use | Usage/service income | Customer terms, support and verified entitlement |
| Licence | Operating capability, funding and agreed fees | Initial/recurring fee or defined royalty | Assets, scope, duration, reporting, restrictions and support |
| Joint venture/spinout | Negotiated capital, work and governance | Negotiated shares, licence fees or paid support | Governance, asset rights and funding agreements |
| Asset purchase | Agreed purchase price and transfer conditions | Sale proceeds and transition fees | Exact assets/exclusions, permissions, acceptance and handover |

No fixed fee, royalty percentage or equity split is adopted here. Price the actual offer using cost, customer value, maturity, rights and support obligations. Define taxes, payment milestones, royalty base if used, refunds, reporting and termination. Revenue is not profit; grants may be restricted; equity may never produce a return.

## Contributor benefits

Record agreed learning objectives, supervision, recognition, expenses and any pay. Equity or revenue participation requires a separate explicit agreement; it is not automatic because someone suggested an idea, worked unpaid, led a team or funded equipment. Do not promise academic credit without institutional agreement.

## Commercial application and handover

Applicant supplies identity/authority, project and assets requested, intended use, users/territory, relevant capability, resources, timing and proposed arrangement. Screen → controlled evaluation → rights and technical due diligence → term sheet → authorised contract → payment/readiness conditions → asset inventory and acceptance → training/support → access review and monitoring.

Commercial access or payment does not itself transfer ownership. The company can grant only rights it holds or is authorised to grant. Keep teacher data and third-party assets out of any transfer unless separately authorised and permitted. This is an operating proposal, not a binding assignment or licence; obtain qualified project-specific review before signing commercial or ownership documents.
