## Session routine

Choose one task and its output. Work within your recorded available time. End by committing relevant changes, linking evidence, recording blockers and choosing the next action. Each week review scope, costs, risks, findings and the next milestone. Do not create retrospective interviews or approvals to make the process look complete.

## Pilot measures

- Preparation time: comparable manual versus assisted task, including review and correction.
- Marking quality: compare against teacher-reviewed reference decisions; measure errors and disagreements by question type. Report uncertain cases rather than hide them in an overall average.
- Identity and completeness: no unnoticed student swaps, missing pages or duplicated records in the evaluated set.
- Totals and export: exact agreement between approved marks, publication and exported totals.
- Usability: completed tasks, assistance needed, failures and teacher feedback.
- Operating cost and reliability: provider cost, latency, failures, support effort and restoration evidence.

Agree numerical quality and time targets with the reviewer after baseline measurement. Report test size and limits. Stop the pilot for identity exposure, lost records, unapproved provider disclosure, unsafe handling or materially unreliable totals; preserve evidence and require a recorded restart decision.

## Release checklist

- [ ] Critical defects closed and rechecked.
- [ ] Teacher review remains required before final marks.
- [ ] Provider/device/deployment checks completed in intended environment.
- [ ] Data permissions, retention and support route confirmed.
- [ ] Restore drill passed; access restrictions checked.
- [ ] Costs and budget approved; paid launch waits for verified billing controls.
- [ ] Pilot report and release decision signed off in the correct capacity.
