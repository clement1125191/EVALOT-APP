# Plans and billing V1 rollout

The supplied product policy is a 45-day free trial with configurable student-paper credits, followed by pay-as-you-go with no recurring educator subscription. One paper means one student's complete script. Trial days and trial credits are independent. Only excess papers are chargeable while eligible free credits remain. Proposed rates apply to the total submitted job size: 1-45 at 30 RWF, 46-100 at 25 RWF, 101+ at 20 RWF. School-funded jobs should not consume personal free credits.

Implemented now: the Plans & billing page presents this offer clearly as pending; the authenticated policy API reads deployment-configurable rates and optional trial allowance, with no hard-coded promise of 30 credits. A validated estimate API applies min(remaining credits, submitted students) before calculating paid papers. Estimates are informational, not authorizations. It does not start paid processing, activate a trial, debit a balance or create invoices. No payment secrets are accepted through the frontend.

Before billing activation: implement workspace-activation timestamps and verification, transactional credit reservations and ledger entries (including retries/failures/refunds), an authenticated admin policy editor, verified school membership and spending approval, signed provider webhooks with idempotency, invoices/receipts and transaction history, and server-side entitlement enforcement across every OCR/AI processing entry point. Existing processing remains pilot access; it is NOT a billing-enforced paid deployment. No checkout or school-code form is offered as functional yet. Choose and configure a payment provider before enabling real collection.

Configuration: BILLING_TRIAL_PAPERS is optional and unset by default; BILLING_RATE_SMALL_RWF, BILLING_RATE_MEDIUM_RWF and BILLING_RATE_LARGE_RWF override the proposed defaults. These configure the displayed offer and estimator, not account entitlement. Keep billingActive and checkoutEnabled false until the enforcement and payment work above is complete.
